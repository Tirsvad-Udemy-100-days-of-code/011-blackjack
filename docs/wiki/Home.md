# Blackjack

A console Blackjack game: play against the computer dealer with emoji cards and try to get closer to 21 than the dealer.

## What is it?

Day 11 of the Udemy course *100 Days of Code: The Complete Python Pro Bootcamp*. You and the computer dealer each get two cards, shown as emoji. You choose to draw another card (`y`) or to pass (`n`), then the dealer draws while its score is below 17 and the program tells you who won. Afterwards you can play again. The deck is unlimited, a jack, queen or king counts 10, an ace counts 11 or 1, and an ace with a ten-card in the first two cards is a blackjack.

## What the exercise teaches

- Writing small functions with parameters and return values
- Keeping the game rules as pure functions apart from console input and output, so they can be tested
- Using lists, loops and conditions to deal cards, score a hand and let the dealer play
- Keeping texts and numbers in constants and writing small automated tests

## What it does

- One player against a computer dealer with an unlimited deck
- Emoji cards and the course's ASCII logo at the start of every game
- Ace counts 11 or 1, and a two-card ace with a ten-card is a blackjack
- The dealer draws while its score is below 17
- Invalid answers are asked for again, and you can play again after each game
